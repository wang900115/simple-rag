# VPN Connection Guide (sample document)

## Installation

1. Download the VPN client from the IT portal.
2. After installing, enter `vpn.example.internal` as the server address.

## Sign-in

Sign in with your company AD username and password, then complete two-factor authentication with the Authenticator app on your phone.

## FAQ

- Connection timeout: make sure your current network does not block UDP port 443.
- Authentication failed: passwords must be changed every 90 days. If yours has expired, reset it on the AD self-service portal first.
