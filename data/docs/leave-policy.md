# Leave Policy (sample document)

## Annual Leave

Employees get 3 days of annual leave after six months of service, 7 days after one year, and 10 days after two years.
Annual leave must be requested in the HR system at least 3 working days in advance and takes effect once the direct manager approves it.

## Sick Leave

Sick leave may total up to 30 days per year, paid at half salary.
Sick leave of 3 or more consecutive days requires a medical certificate.

## Personal Leave

Personal leave may total up to 14 days per year and is unpaid.
